Who we are

{{ site_name }} is run by {{ legal.operator_name(operator) }} ("we"). It turns files of eSIM activation codes into branded PDFs with QR codes. Contact: {{ legal.contact_link(contact) }}.

Two roles

  • Account data (your login, your organization, the records listed below): we decide how it is used, so we are the controller.
  • The files you upload (activation codes, ICCIDs, confirmation codes, customer names and emails): your company is the controller. We act as your processor and use this data only to make your PDFs. If you received an eSIM from one of our customers, contact that company about your data.

What we store

  • Accounts: email address, organization, role (owner or member), whether the account is an administrator or disabled, when it was created and last signed in. Passwords are stored only as an argon2id hash.
  • Sessions: only hashes of the session and form-protection tokens.
  • Two-factor authentication: the authenticator secret, encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Recovery codes are stored only as keyed hashes (HMAC). Neither is shown again after setup.
  • Invitation and password links: a hash of the link, its expiry and whether it was used. For invitations, also the invited email address, organization and role.
  • Access requests: company name, your name, work email, expected monthly volume and your optional note.
  • Audit log: security events (sign-ins, invitations, changes to accounts and batches). Each entry has the user and organization IDs, the action, the result, the time and the first part of the IP address (an IP prefix: /{{ ipv4_prefix }} for IPv4, /{{ ipv6_prefix }} for IPv6). It never has email addresses, passwords or codes.
  • Batches: for each upload that passed the check, the file name, row and part counts, warning and error counts, status, who uploaded it and when, and whether it was voided and by whom.
  • Fingerprints: a keyed hash (HMAC-SHA256) of each ICCID and activation code that was turned into a PDF, so the same eSIM is not issued twice. The codes cannot be recovered from these.
  • Usage: the number of rows generated per organization per day, for the daily limit.

What we never store

Uploaded files, activation codes, confirmation codes, PDFs and ZIP files, customer names and customer emails, and the branding you enter (company name, colour, footer text, logo). None of these are written to disk, to logs or to the database.

Kept in memory only

After a file passes the check, its rows (including the activation codes) stay in the server's memory so a large order can download in parts. They are deleted {{ legal.p(period, "upload_cache") }} after they were last used, or sooner: when every part has been downloaded, when generation fails, when the batch is voided, when memory runs short, or when the server restarts.

Who processes data for us

  • Render (render.com): hosting, database and request logs, in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Resend (resend.com): sends account emails from its EU region, only when email sending is switched on.
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com): DNS for esimsheet.com, and forwarding of mail sent to {{ legal.contact_link(contact) }}. Traffic to the app itself does not pass through Cloudflare.
  • Google (Gmail): the mailbox that receives the forwarded mail.

Cloudflare and Google are US companies and may process data outside the EU.

Emails we send

  • Invitation: your email address, a one-time link, the organization name and your role.
  • Password reset: your email address and a one-time link.
  • New access request (to us only): the company name and expected volume.

Emails have no images and no tracking. They never contain activation codes or anything from your uploads.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its use or to receive it in a portable form, and you can object to how we use it. Write to {{ legal.contact_link(contact) }}. You can also complain to your data protection authority. For data in files a company uploaded about you, please contact that company first.

Security

The site works only over HTTPS. Passwords, links, recovery codes and code fingerprints are stored only as hashes, and two-factor secrets only encrypted. Two-factor sign-in is available to everyone and required for administrators.

Changes

When this policy changes we update this page and its date, and we email account owners about important changes.
